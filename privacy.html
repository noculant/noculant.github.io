1. Introduction

Last Updated: January 17, 2025

Welcome to NOC ("we," "us," "our," or "the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By accessing or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the App.

We reserve the right to make changes to this Privacy Policy at any time. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date and, where required by law, by providing additional notice through the App or via email.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, information that is automatically collected when you use the App, and information from third-party sources.

2.1 Personal Information You Provide

Account Registration Information:

  • Email address (required for account creation and verification)
  • Password (encrypted and stored securely)
  • First and last name

Profile Information:

  • Profile picture/photo
  • Gender (optional)
  • Date of birth (optional)
  • Academic information including:
    • Educational institution/campus
    • Degree level (Bachelor's, Master's, etc.)
    • Course/program of study
    • Batch/year
    • Roll number (optional)

User-Generated Content:

  • Posts (text and images)
  • Comments on posts and events
  • Event creation and participation data
  • Club membership and leadership information
  • Likes and reactions to content
  • Reported content and blocking preferences

Communication Data:

  • Support requests and correspondence
  • Feedback and survey responses

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

Device Information:

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system and version (iOS or Android)
  • Unique device identifiers
  • Mobile network information
  • Device settings and preferences

Usage Information:

  • App features accessed and used
  • Time and date of app usage
  • Login frequency and session duration
  • Content viewed and interactions
  • Navigation paths through the App
  • Performance and error logs

Location Information:

  • Campus location (selected during registration)
  • Event locations (as text, not GPS coordinates)
  • Note: We do NOT collect precise geolocation data or GPS coordinates

Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) Token:

  • Device-specific push notification token
  • Used exclusively for sending notifications

Local Storage Data:

  • App preferences and settings
  • Cached content for offline access
  • Redux state data (stored locally on your device)

2.3 Information from Third-Party Services

Firebase Services:

  • We use Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications
  • Firebase may collect analytics and performance data
  • Subject to Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy)

Calendar Integration:

  • When you choose to add events to your calendar, we access your device calendar with your explicit permission
  • We do not store or transmit calendar data to our servers

Photo Library and Camera:

  • When you upload images, we access your photo library or camera with your explicit permission
  • Images are uploaded to our servers only when you explicitly choose to post them

3. How We Use Your Information

3.1 Providing Core Services

  • Account Management: Create, maintain, and secure your account
  • Authentication: Verify your identity through email OTP verification
  • Profile Management: Display and update your profile information
  • Content Delivery: Show you relevant posts, events, and club information
  • Social Features: Enable interactions through likes, comments, and follows
  • Event Management: Create, manage, and participate in campus events
  • Club Management: Join clubs, manage memberships, and handle leadership roles

3.2 Communication

  • Push Notifications: activity on your posts/events, club updates, event reminders, new followers, system updates
  • Email Communication: OTP verification, password resets, service announcements, policy updates

3.3 Personalization and Improvement

  • Customize your feed based on campus, clubs, interests
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve features and UX
  • Identify and resolve technical issues
  • Enhance app speed and reliability

3.4 Safety and Security

  • Moderate reported content and enforce guidelines
  • Detect/prevent fraudulent or unauthorized activity
  • Investigate Terms of Service violations
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations

3.5 Analytics and Research

  • Understand how users interact with the App
  • Measure feature adoption/effectiveness
  • Generate anonymized usage insights

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

4.1 With Other Users (By Design)

  • Public profile details visible to your campus
  • Posts, comments, likes/reactions visible to other users
  • Club membership and event participation visibility

4.2 Service Providers

  • Firebase/Google Cloud: push notifications & cloud infrastructure
  • Hosting Providers: server hosting & data storage (https://noc-api.dextrouslab.com/)
  • Image Storage: profile, post, and event images

Providers are contractually obligated to protect your information and use it only for specified purposes.

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or to protect rights, property, or safety of NOC, users, or others.

4.4 Business Transfers

If NOC is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information as long as necessary to provide services and for the purposes in this policy.

5.1 Account Deletion

  • Personal information deleted/anonymized within 30 days
  • Some data retained for legitimate business purposes
  • Backups generally deleted within 90 days
  • Shared content may remain visible; identifying info removed

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical, administrative, and physical security measures.

6.1 Security Measures

  • HTTPS encryption in transit
  • Secure token-based authentication
  • Access controls on a need-to-know basis

6.2 Security Limitations

  • Local storage (e.g., AsyncStorage) is not encrypted by default
  • No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure
  • Users must keep passwords confidential

6.3 Data Breach Notification

If a data breach occurs, affected users will be notified within 72 hours where required by law.
