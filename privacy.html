Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 17, 2025

Introduction

Welcome to NOC ("we," "us," "our," or "the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By accessing or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the App.

We reserve the right to make changes to this Privacy Policy at any time. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date and, where required by law, by providing additional notice through the App or via email.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, information that is automatically collected when you use the App, and information from third-party sources.

1.1 Personal Information You Provide

Account Registration Information:

  • Email address (required for account creation and verification)
  • Password (encrypted and stored securely)
  • First and last name

Profile Information:

  • Profile picture/photo
  • Gender (optional)
  • Date of birth (optional)
  • Academic information including:
    • Educational institution/campus
    • Degree level (Bachelor's, Master's, etc.)
    • Course/program of study
    • Batch/year
    • Roll number (optional)

User-Generated Content:

  • Posts (text and images), comments, events
  • Club membership/leadership
  • Likes/reactions, reported content, blocking prefs

Communication Data:

  • Support requests and correspondence
  • Feedback and survey responses

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

Device Information:

  • Device type/model, OS/version, unique identifiers
  • Mobile network info, device settings/preferences

Usage Information:

  • Features used, timestamps, session duration
  • Content viewed/interactions, navigation paths
  • Performance and error logs

Location Information:

  • Campus selection and event locations (text only)
  • We do NOT collect precise GPS coordinates

FCM Token & Local Storage:

  • Device-specific push token (notifications only)
  • App preferences, cached content, Redux state

1.3 Information from Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Services: push notifications; subject to Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Calendar Integration: access only with permission; no server storage.
  • Photo Library/Camera: access only with permission; uploads only when you choose to post.

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 Providing Core Services

  • Account & authentication (OTP), profiles
  • Feed/content delivery, social features
  • Events & clubs (create, manage, join)

2.2 Communication

  • Push notifications (activity, clubs, reminders)
  • Email (OTP, resets, important updates)

2.3 Personalization & Improvement

  • Personalized feed and suggestions
  • Analytics for UX, performance, bug fixes

2.4 Safety & Security

  • Moderation, fraud prevention, Terms enforcement
  • Legal compliance

2.5 Analytics & Research

  • Usage/feature analytics and aggregate stats

3. How We Share Your Information

3.1 With Other Users (By Design)

  • Public profile details
  • Posts/comments, likes/reactions
  • Club membership and event participation

3.2 Service Providers

  • Firebase/Google Cloud (notifications/infrastructure)
  • Hosting & image storage

All providers are contractually obligated to protect your data.

3.3 Legal Requirements

Disclosures required by law or to protect rights and safety.

3.4 Business Transfers

Your information may transfer in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale.

4. Data Retention

We retain your information as long as necessary to provide services and fulfill the purposes outlined in this Policy.

4.1 Account Deletion

  • Delete or anonymize personal info within 30 days
  • Backups generally deleted within 90 days
  • Some data retained for legitimate purposes
  • Shared content may remain without identifiers

5. Data Security

5.1 Security Measures

  • HTTPS in transit
  • Token-based authentication
  • Role/need-to-know access controls

5.2 Limitations & Breach Notice

  • Local device storage not encrypted by default
  • No method is 100% secure
  • Breach notifications within 72 hours where required

7. Children’s Privacy

Intended for users 13+. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent. If you believe your child provided data, contact us at [Your Contact Email].

8. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in other countries. We implement appropriate safeguards (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses).

9. Regional Privacy Rights

9.1 EEA — GDPR

Bases: Consent, Contract, Legitimate Interests, Legal Obligations. Rights: Access, Rectify, Erase, Restrict, Portability, Object, Complain to DPA.

9.2 California — CCPA/CPRA

Rights to Know, Delete, Correct. We don’t sell personal info or use sensitive info in ways requiring “limit use.” Exercise rights via [Your Contact Email].

9.3 Other U.S. States

Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, etc. may provide similar rights.

10. Third-Party Services and Tracking

10.1 Third-Party Services

Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications. We’re not responsible for external site practices.

10.2 No Tracking Technologies

No browser cookies/ads trackers; local storage only for app state and preferences.

11. Educational Institutions

Independent platform; not sponsored by institutions. We don’t share personal info with institutions without consent unless required by law.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Policy. Material changes will be communicated in-app or by email. Continued use constitutes acceptance.
